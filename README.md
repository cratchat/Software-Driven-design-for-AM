📌 Overview

This repository accompanies the lecture “Software-Driven Design for Additive Manufacturing”. It introduces the theory, workflow, and intuition behind computational-driven design, with a focus on:

Topology Optimization

Generative Design

Inverse Design Thinking

Design Space vs Performance Space

Voxel-based and density-based optimization methods

The material is intended for upper-undergraduate, graduate, and research-level audiences in mechanical engineering, additive manufacturing, robotics, and cyber-physical systems.

🎯 Learning Objectives

After studying this material, students should be able to:

Understand the philosophical shift from forward analysis to inverse design

Distinguish between design space and performance space

Explain how topology optimization and generative design work

Describe common optimization approaches such as SIMP and ESO

Interpret why optimized structures often appear organic and non-intuitive

Relate computational design methods to additive manufacturing advantages
